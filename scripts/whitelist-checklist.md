# Whitelist / New Launch Checklist

A quick DYOR checklist before joining a whitelist or a new token launch.

## Contract

- [ ] Contract source verified on the explorer
- [ ] No hidden mint function, no owner-only drain
- [ ] Buy/sell tax is reasonable and readable
- [ ] Not a honeypot (test with a tiny amount first)

## Liquidity & distribution

- [ ] Liquidity locked or burned
- [ ] Team / insider allocation is small and transparent
- [ ] No single wallet holds a huge share of supply

## Project

- [ ] Real team or at least a track record
- [ ] Public docs, roadmap and working product or demo
- [ ] Community looks real, not bought engagement

## Timing

- [ ] Know the exact launch time and entry rules
- [ ] Have a plan for entry size and exit
- [ ] Never chase after insiders have already sold
