# Awesome Crypto Tools

A short curated list of public tools. No affiliate links, no private keys, DYOR.

## Block explorers

- [Etherscan](https://etherscan.io) — Ethereum explorer
- [BscScan](https://bscscan.com) — BNB Chain explorer
- [Solscan](https://solscan.io) — Solana explorer
- [Basescan](https://basescan.org) — Base explorer

## Trading & DEX

- [Dexscreener](https://dexscreener.com) — DEX pairs, charts and trending tokens
- [DEXTools](https://www.dextools.io) — pair explorer and live trading data
- [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org) — the biggest DEX on Ethereum and L2s
- [PancakeSwap](https://pancakeswap.finance) — the biggest DEX on BNB Chain

## Analytics

- [Dune](https://dune.com) — community dashboards and on-chain queries
- [DefiLlama](https://defillama.com) — TVL and DeFi analytics
- [Arkham](https://platform.arkhamintelligence.com) — wallet labels and entity tracking
- [Nansen](https://www.nansen.ai) — smart money and wallet analytics

## Wallets

- [MetaMask](https://metamask.io) — the classic EVM browser wallet
- [Phantom](https://phantom.com) — Solana wallet
- [Rabby](https://rabby.io) — EVM wallet with pre-transaction checks

## Launchpads

- [Pump.fun](https://pump.fun) — Solana memecoin launchpad
- [Four.meme](https://four.meme) — BNB Chain memecoin launchpad
- [TokenFi](https://tokenfi.com) — token launchpad

## Security

- [Revoke.cash](https://revoke.cash) — check and revoke token approvals
- [De.Fi Scanner](https://de.fi/scanner) — free smart contract audit scanner
- [Honeypot.is](https://honeypot.is) — honeypot and tax checker
